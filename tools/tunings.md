The 100-level campaign was designed by `node tools/design-levels.mjs` for conveyor boarding (people hop on at the bottom of the ring). Each level is packed into a narrow chevron jam of 10–13 buses so parked buses stay around 40 px wide on an iPhone SE. Candidates are kept only if the official solver can win them (replayed with the real engine), most buses start blocked, bay pressure stays at or under 4, mystery buses start blocked, and buses, colours, mystery buses, blocked count and bay pressure never drop. A few neighbouring levels share a car park and differ only in the queue. No hand edits of the JSON after the designer and uniquify pass.
